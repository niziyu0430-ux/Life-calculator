# Life Counter asset register

Seven original 1536 × 1024 illustrations were generated for six articles and the planner. Full prompts, thematic references and unique assignments: [assets/original-illustrations.md](assets/original-illustrations.md).

The earlier Adobe-adjusted photos and old hourglass image are removed from the published tree. The photo directory remains a local reference collection and is not published. No signature was removed. Chinese and English counterpart pages share their assigned artwork; article lists contain no duplicated artwork.

The warm garden illustration, public/images/time-garden.webp, remains in the home planning feature only. It was created with built-in image generation: a miniature winding botanical paper garden, cream #FBF7F2, sand, pale apricot #F2E5D8, terracotta #96543E; tactile material, soft daylight, no lettering or logo.

## Three-dimensional characters

Original Blender 4.5.8 mesh sculpts authored by assets/models/build_characters.py. Two characters, seven morph targets (1, 8, 16, 25, 45, 65, 85), a skin and Idle_Breathe bone animation per GLB. Character resources: public/models/character-0.glb (1,933,204 bytes), character-1.glb (2,078,608 bytes). Combined 4,011,812 bytes. WebP posters are exported views of these models, not replacements for 3D. Character assets are reused by functional home and game components. Metadata: assets/models/metadata.json. Authoring source can be regenerated using the portable Blender in work/blender; blend files are retained in the local visualization output folder.

## Fonts

Official Noto Sans CJK SC Regular / Medium and Noto Serif CJK SC SemiBold from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk. Local WOFF2 subsets use scripts/subset-fonts.py. License texts remain under public/fonts. Regenerate after content additions.
