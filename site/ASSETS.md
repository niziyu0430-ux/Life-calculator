# Illustration provenance

Created with built-in image generation for this project, then resized only by lossless-dimension WebP format optimization (1536 × 1024). No external stock images were used for these two illustrations.

- `public/images/time-garden.webp`: winding ivory steps through a miniature botanical garden; forest green, sage and lime paper sculpture. Original: life-counter-garden.png.
- `public/images/time-hourglass.webp`: sculptural hourglass and blank calendar cards beneath a sunlit archway. Original: life-counter-hourglass.png.

Generation briefs: premium landscape editorial 3D papercraft, tactile paper grain, soft bright sunlight, optimistic serene atmosphere, forest green #16382f, ivory, sage and restrained lime #d9f47c. Garden composition: human-free miniature winding stepped path through sculptural layered botanical foliage with balanced breathing room. Hourglass composition: tiny gold grains, curved emerald plinth, blank ivory calendar stack, geometric archway and single botanical branch. Both: no people, text, numbers, logos, watermark or UI.

## Warm edition

The two existing illustrations were recolored using built-in image generation, preserving the subjects and layout. Edit brief: cream #FBF7F2, sand, pale apricot #F2E5D8 and terracotta #96543E; replace green tones; preserve composition, paper/ceramic textures, lighting and object shapes; no text, logos or additional objects. Originals remain outside the published tree.

User-supplied photos were processed with Adobe image_apply_adjustments: tempA 3, tempB 9, tempLuminance 50, saturation -12, highlights -10. The first output was inspected before processing the other two; all outputs were visually inspected. Full framing was retained because the subjects already fit the editorial placement. WebP copies are format optimizations of these outputs.
